The clutter was redundancy — the same fact in several visual languages. Resources spoke a different row language from issues; the rollup said "1 blocked" as a tile, a legend entry and a callout; tab-level pages printed an h1 identical to the tab beneath them.

The activity feed was a log rather than a story: atomic events, the actor's name on every row, no sense of when. It now collapses consecutive events by one actor on one node into a beat, groups beats by day, and leads with the node.
